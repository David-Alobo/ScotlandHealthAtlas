# Music in the video tour

**The Mist Covered Mountains** (*Chì mi na mòrbheanna*), a traditional Scottish Gaelic air,
performed by the Concert Band of the United States Air Force Band (track 5 of *Heritage to
Horizons*, 2007). Public domain, as a work of the United States government.
Source: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Mist_Covered_Mountains_-_Concert_Band_-_United_States_Air_Force_Band.mp3),
downloaded 14 November 2026 (`mist-covered-mountains.mp3`, unchanged).

In the video it plays quietly under the tour: two copies joined with a three-second crossfade,
levelled to -20 LUFS, faded in and out (`web/tools/video.sh`).
