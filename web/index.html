      Methods and code

      What this can and cannot say

      • Deprivation describes neighbourhoods, not each person's circumstances.
      • A&E figures count visits, not people.
      • Council comparisons compare areas; they do not show that deprived people are the ones who die.
      • Prescriptions are counted where they are dispensed, not always where people live.
      • The injury figures reproduce Public Health Scotland's published totals (deaths exactly, admissions within 2%).

      How this was made

      • Rates are standardised for age and sex: to Scotland's 2024 population for A&E, to the 2013 European Standard Population for injuries (as Public Health Scotland does), with 95% confidence intervals. Councils are "clearly above" or "below" Scotland when their interval excludes Scotland's rate.
      • Deprivation is the Scottish Index of Multiple Deprivation 2020v2. Populations are National Records of Scotland small area estimates.
      • Checks: the injury figures reproduce PHS's published totals; a narrowing A&E gap was tested and not claimed; fewer than 20 deaths are not shown as a rate.
      • Use of AI. The analysis code, charts and this page were built with the help of an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic), directed and reviewed by the author. Every number on the page is read from the saved analysis tables in the repository, and the code, tests and tables are public.
